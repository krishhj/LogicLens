💡 About Sentence & Text Processing

Overview

Sentence algorithms process words and character tokens for NLP analysis, sliding window substring evaluation, frequency counting, anagram grouping, and encryption ciphers.

  • Longest Substring Without Repeats: Maintains a sliding window $[L, R]$ with a character-index map.
  • Anagram Check & Grouping: Compares character frequency histograms and groups words by sorted character keys.
  • Word Frequency Counter: Reads word tokens and builds a dynamic Hash Map frequency table.
  • Longest Common Prefix: Compares character columns across all input words simultaneously.
  • Reverse Words: Performs a two-step reversal (reverses full string, then reverses each individual word).
  • Longest Palindromic Substring: Expands around all odd and even centers to locate the maximal palindrome range.
  • Caesar Cipher & RLE: Aligns input characters with output character shifts and run-length frequency pairs.
